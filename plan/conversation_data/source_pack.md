**Answerability boundary:** A question about a *proposed* amount above is answerable with its status caveat. A question asking whether a person is eligible, what exact limit/rate they will get, whether a named lounge is included, whether a trip is insured, what current FX quote applies, or what the total cost will be is **not answerable from this snapshot**. The right response may combine one known fact with an explicit unknown and a handoff offer.

For a **dated historical** miles calculation, the answer source also needs the exact direct currency→USD row from the supplied `data/daily_exchange_rates.csv`. That row is runtime tool evidence (`FX.HISTORICAL`), not a searchable offer fact or a current exchange-rate quote. If the row is missing, the advisor cannot give an exact miles figure.
