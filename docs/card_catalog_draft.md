# Draft demo credit-card catalog

**Status: planning draft, 2026-09-28.** These are team-created offers, not products or terms supplied by the organizer. No card can be marketed as a real bank offer, no numeric recommendation rule is approved, and no application can be approved by the advisor. The English names are stable product identities, not lettered or numbered ranks; another card can be inserted later without renaming them. The same brand names are used in Spanish and Portuguese conversations. This demo serves **existing bank customers** only: a customer must match a trusted session and a row in `CORE.CUSTOMERS` before receiving a card suggestion, simulated precheck, or mock application. This is a chosen demo scope, not a general banking-industry requirement.

| Stable ID / display name | Designed for | Proposed distinguishing features | Recommendation guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CAMPUS` / **Campus** | **Students only** | No annual fee; spending controls and payment reminders; modest starting limit; proposed education and everyday-spend rewards | Suggest only to customers whose supplied segment is `Student`. The dataset has no verified school-enrollment field, so any student-only requirement needs human verification before a real decision. |
| `HORIZON` / **Horizon** | Non-student customers seeking a straightforward entry card for routine purchases | No annual fee; proposed rewards on groceries and transit; spending alerts; standard support | The entry option for non-students who are not suited to a higher-benefit card; do not automatically offer it as a fallback after a failed precheck. |
| `REWARDS` / **Rewards** | Mid-tier customers who value a mix of everyday rewards and some travel benefits | Proposed 1 mile per USD-equivalent unit of all posted retail purchases, 2 lounge visits per year, travel coverage up to USD 20,000, and a moderate possible limit | Mid-tier placement depends on country-specific income and score rules plus customer preferences; its lounge, insurance, and miles benefits must be more limited than Summit's. |
| `SUMMIT` / **Summit** | Customers seeking premium service and able to consider higher costs | Proposed 2 miles per USD-equivalent unit of all posted retail purchases, 8 lounge visits per year, travel coverage up to USD 75,000, priority support, and a higher possible limit | Explain costs and trade-offs; never infer suitability or approval from wealth alone. |

The features above are **concepts**, not finalized customer-facing promises. The same core benefits are planned in Colombia, México, and Argentina; fees and required disclosures use country-specific versions. The user accepted the [synthetic price draft](../plan/card_offer_terms.md), the USD-based all-purchase miles rule, travel quantities as **proposed benefits**, and no minimum holding/payment period for any card, with cancellation available at any time without an early-cancellation penalty. The [travel-rule draft](../plan/card_offer_terms.md#draft-usd-conversion-rule-for-miles) now defines guest use, trip-purchase requirements, duration, emergency-medical coverage, and basic exclusions; partner names, named lounges, and issued coverage remain unspecified. A [historical miles-conversion method](../plan/card_offer_terms.md#draft-usd-conversion-rule-for-miles) is drafted, but no current FX feed exists for exact new-purchase miles. Each card needs a versioned, country-specific offer record before an LLM or UI can state precise terms: offer ID and display name, allowed country/currency, active dates, annual fee and spend-based waiver terms where applicable, purchase interest with the correct local rate basis, credit-limit range, rewards and exclusions, travel/FX terms, repayment/grace-period terms, support features, eligibility-policy reference, required disclosures, and source/version for each claim. Taxes, cash-advance and late fees, México CAT, and Argentina CFT are unavailable in the demo; the advisor must disclose that limitation and hand off full-cost questions. The model must not fill in unknown terms.

## Income and recommendation inputs

The organizer's [complete data dictionary](factored_docs/complete_data_dictonary.pdf) describes `CUSTOMERS.estimated_monthly_income` as **estimated monthly income in local currency**. The CSV has no income-currency column. Its `country` values include Colombia, México, and Argentina, with very different income magnitudes. **All three countries are in scope for the demo.** A country-to-currency mapping (Colombia → COP, México → MXN, Argentina → ARS) and separate recommendation bands per country must be explicit and validated before numeric thresholds are chosen. Do not compare raw income numbers across countries or silently treat them as USD. Customer credit score is documented on a 300–850 scale.

The advisor should separate **public catalog information**, **suggestions to existing customers**, and the later **simulated precheck**. After a trusted demo sign-in establishes an existing bank customer and separate permission allows score/income use, a versioned recommendation policy may rank a small set of cards using validated country, credit score, monthly income, existing card holdings, segment, and stated preferences. This is a suggestion, not an eligibility or approval result. The `Student` segment can support a Campus suggestion but does not prove enrollment. A customer may ask about any card, including one the advisor did not suggest; asking does not waive card-specific rules. If score, income, or currency is missing or ambiguous, ask or provide non-personalized information rather than invent a value.

Visitors may type their monthly income. Capture amount, currency, and whether it is self-reported; do not silently substitute it for a stored estimate. **Anonymous income never triggers a card suggestion.** After sign-in and permission, show any material difference between typed and stored estimates and send the final decision to a human. Do not access stored income/score before trusted sign-in and specific permission.

## Conversation entry

- **Campaign card:** The judge clicks a visible card in the demo UI, creating a *new simulated click* and conversation. Selected supplied historical campaign rows are mapped explicitly to one named demo offer apiece; that mapping is team-created and labeled because the source only says `Tarjeta Crédito`. The assistant opens with general information about the selected card, then invites sign-in and separate permission before making any personal suggestion. A new demo click is not a historical `CAMPAIGN_SENDS.was_clicked` event; no historical clicked chat is selected.
- **Direct visit:** The judge opens the advisor without campaign context. The opening is generic and invites a product question or public catalog browsing. A card suggestion waits for trusted sign-in as an existing customer and separate permission.

Both paths should preserve entry source and selected offer in the conversation state. Neither a campaign ID nor a click authenticates a customer. The LLM may eventually handle free-form dialogue, questions, and tone, but the trusted session, consent, offer facts, recommendation policy, and action state remain controlled outside the model. The translated transcript [tone examples](../data/derived/tone_examples_es_pt.json) may serve as a style reference; they do not contain product knowledge and should not be retrieved as factual card-answer passages.
