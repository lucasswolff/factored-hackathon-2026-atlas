# Training-data assessment for the credit-product advisor

**Current priority: decide what can be learned from the supplied data before extending the MVP.** This is an exploratory audit, not a trained model or a claim of production readiness. The local CSVs and Snowflake tables came from the same AWS source. Counts below were obtained from aggregate DuckDB scans of the local CSVs with values read as strings and appropriate joins to campaigns, customers, or products.

**User-selected priority: better product conversations.** Click prediction is a possible secondary data-science task, not the model to prioritize for the advisor.

## Which learning targets are actually supported?

| Candidate target | Supplied inputs and labels | Judgment for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Predict a campaign click** | `CAMPAIGN_SENDS` has 1,746,801 sends and 97,793 `was_clicked=true` labels; campaign, channel, template, and send time are available. Acquisition campaigns promoting credit cards have 84,838 sends and 4,921 clicks. | **Potentially trainable**, but this predicts engagement, not eligibility, conversation quality, or product acquisition. Validate channel measurement and as-of features first. |
| **Predict a product contract from a campaign** | `had_conversion` appears on 9,799 sends, but the dictionary calls it completion of a desired action. Only two flagged conversions coincide with a promoted-product opening on the recorded conversion date. | **No trustworthy contract label or attribution.** Do not train or report a campaign-to-acquisition model from this flag. |
| **Classify credit-product questions or retrieve answers** | 171,321 call transcripts have only 42 distinct customer-text strings; all are balance questions in Spanish. `detected_intents` has one non-null value. Credit-card campaign descriptions are generic (52 campaigns, five distinct non-null descriptions). | **Supplied text is not suitable training or answer-source material** for the proposed advisor. A versioned offer/FAQ and independently judged Spanish/Portuguese questions must be created. Prefer evaluating a pretrained multilingual component before claiming a newly trained model helps. |
| **Predict eligibility or approval** | `CUSTOMERS` has credit scores and estimated income; `PRODUCTS` has existing holdings and limits. There are no application decisions, approved eligibility rules, or underwriting outcomes. | **Not trainable or validatable from these files.** Existing ownership is not an eligibility label. Any demo precheck remains a labeled synthetic, deterministic policy. |
| **Predict who starts chat or applies after clicking** | Call-center interactions have no send/campaign ID; digital events have generic UTM values and no product application events. | **No reliable end-to-end label.** Customer/time proximity is not enough to establish campaign attribution. |

The earlier synthetic prototype was removed. Its team-written FAQ examples and small offline score did not show that organizer data could train a production-quality advisor. A new offer, policy, and independently judged question set still need to be designed.

A further text-source sweep found no hidden product FAQ: 212,759 satisfaction surveys contain only 13 distinct non-null open-comment values (none mention a card), 67,095 complaints have five distinct descriptions, and 15,620,994 digital events have only three non-null product page titles (`Préstamos`, `Tarjeta de Crédito`, `Cuenta de Ahorro`). These sources can describe demand and navigation, but cannot supply offer terms or varied customer questions.

For tone only, the 171,321 transcripts contain **42 distinct Spanish agent-text strings**. They are combinations of just two balance-answer templates and four closing phrases. The [42 Spanish/Portuguese pairs](../data/derived/tone_examples_es_pt.json) were extracted reproducibly by [a preparation script](../analysis/prepare_tone_examples.py); Portuguese is team-translated and awaits bilingual review. This is a style reference, not 42 independent conversational examples or Portuguese ground truth. A chat assistant should disclose that it is AI while being clear and courteous.

## Conversation-training plan: research first

1. **Define the task before choosing a model.** Separate (a) routing a user turn to offer information, clarification, simulated precheck, application request, or human review; (b) retrieving the exact offer section needed; and (c) generating a grounded answer. A single FAQ category label does not evaluate all three.
2. **Build an answer source.** The current versioned synthetic offer/FAQ may be the initial corpus, but every rate, fee, benefit, eligibility statement, and expiry must have an explicit source section and version. Supplied customer product rates are not offer terms.
3. **Create an annotation guide and independent judgments.** For each Spanish and Portuguese test conversation, record the utterance, relevant prior turns, language, expected route, relevant offer section(s), required facts, whether clarification/handoff is needed, and provenance. Include colloquial phrasing, code-switching, vague questions, unsupported claims, missing context, and prompt injection. Have a second bilingual reviewer judge a sample and resolve disagreements before model selection.
4. **Avoid near-duplicate leakage.** Split cases by intent/scenario and paraphrase family; keep the final independently written test set untouched. Report results by language and by question type, not one overall accuracy number.
5. **Compare useful baselines.** Start with exact/keyword matching and a general multilingual pretrained retrieval or classification component. Fine-tune only if the labeled set is large and varied enough to justify it. Evaluate routing accuracy, retrieval relevance, factual grounding, abstention/handoff, and unsafe answers on the same held-out cases. The deterministic eligibility policy remains outside the learned component.

The [conversation-data package](../plan/conversation_data/README.md) now contains a versioned evaluation fact sheet, annotation protocol, and 24 team-written draft cases (12 Spanish, 12 Portuguese). A [development-only keyword versus pretrained multilingual E5 retrieval comparison](../plan/conversation_data/dev_retrieval_report.md), a [development Sonnet answer check](../plan/conversation_data/dev_answer_report_v2.md), a [provisional AI-only answer review](../plan/conversation_data/pilot_ai_review_v2.md), and an [AI-authored/AI-judged challenge](../plan/conversation_data/ai_challenge_report.md) have been run. Independent human bilingual validation and a truly external held-out set remain unfulfilled; the AI challenge cannot substitute for those claims. This is data work, not another MVP feature. The organizer data still contributes campaign context and realistic customer-field distributions for test coverage; it does not become conversational ground truth merely because it is large.

## Important label and feature checks

- Click/open tracking differs sharply by channel. Across all sends, Email has 35,183 recorded clicks, SMS 40,609, and Push 22,001. **WhatsApp has 349,144 sends and Voice 54,529, with zero recorded opens and clicks for both.** Treat these as unmeasured engagement channels, not proof that no recipients engaged. An all-channel click model could learn this instrumentation artifact.
- For Acquisition campaigns promoting credit cards, SMS click rate is 9.56%, Push 7.21%, Email 5.50%, and WhatsApp 0% as recorded. Restrict an initial click experiment to channels with observable clicks and state whether the prediction is made before send or after confirmed delivery.
- Customer and product tables are snapshots, not feature histories. For 432,119 of 1,746,801 sends, `CUSTOMERS.LAST_UPDATED` is later than `SEND_DATE`; other snapshot values may also reflect later changes. Do not join current credit score, income, segment, or product ownership to old sends and assume those values were known then.
- 874,417 sends join to a customer whose current `ACCEPTS_MARKETING` value is false. This is another reason not to treat the snapshot as a historical consent record or use these sends to authorize new outreach.
- Campaign `was_opened`, `open_date`, `was_delivered`, `was_clicked`, `click_date`, `had_conversion`, and conversion fields occur after send. They must not be features for a **send-time** click model. `template_used`, campaign attributes, channel, send time, and prior interactions computed strictly before each send are candidate features, subject to validation.
- Existing card ownership is a poor substitute for eligibility. In the local snapshot, credit-card owners and non-owners have nearly identical mean credit scores (647.0 versus 647.2); more importantly, owning a product does not reveal whether a person applied, qualified, was offered it, or was denied.

## Recommended data-first sequence

1. **Prioritize the selected conversation task.** Write the question/route/relevance label schema and annotation guide, then create an independently reviewed multilingual pilot. Do not force the campaign-click label into an eligibility or answer-quality claim.
2. **Define dataset contracts.** For the conversation task, record offer version, provenance, language, context, label definitions, and split family. If a later click model is pursued, separately record population, outcome, observation time, allowed pre-outcome features, missingness, and channel exclusions.
3. **Freeze evaluation before modeling.** Use a chronological holdout, with all historical features computed as of each send. Also report performance on customers or campaigns not seen in training if generalization to them is intended. For advisor questions, split by scenario/paraphrase family and language so near-duplicates do not cross train/test boundaries.
4. **Compare with simple baselines.** For click propensity, compare a constant rate and past campaign/channel rates with a learned model using appropriate imbalance-aware metrics and calibration. Report results by channel and country, plus sample sizes. For product Q&A, compare keyword retrieval with a multilingual pretrained retriever or classifier on independently judged relevance/intent labels; include out-of-scope and ambiguous questions.
5. **Make a go/no-go decision.** Continue the credit advisor with honest provenance: organizer data for campaign context and baselines, a team-created offer/policy, and separately labeled conversational training/evaluation. If the goal changes to having organizer data train the main model, campaign-response prediction would need a larger role; it must not be presented as an eligibility model.

This research phase does not change the [future MVP requirements](mvp_requirements.md). It determines which learned component and claims those requirements can credibly support. A/B testing remains deferred.
